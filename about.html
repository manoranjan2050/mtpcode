Mission

MTPCode exists to build software that real businesses can trust with their day-to-day operations — not demos, not MVPs that get abandoned, but tools that stay running: a pharmacy's inventory, a shop's daily bills, a trader's live positions. Every product on this site is either in production use or actively being shipped toward it.

Trading Systems

IoT & Firmware
