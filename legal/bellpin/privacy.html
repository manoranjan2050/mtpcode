BellPin Privacy Policy

BellPin – Student Toolkit · Last updated September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP CODE ("we", "us") handles information in connection with BellPin (package com.mtpcode.bellpin), an Android app that combines a class timetable, attendance tracker, lecture recorder, scratchpad, and homework planner for students.

Website: https://mtpcode.com
Support: admin@mtpcode.com

1. Overview

BellPin is built to work entirely offline. There is no sign-up and no server operated by us — the app never sends your data to MTP CODE. Every core feature (timetable, attendance, homework, notes, lecture recordings, blackboard scan) runs and stores its data only on your device. The only exception is the optional "BellPin Pro" Google Drive Backup feature described in Section 3, which you must explicitly turn on.

2. Data stored on your device

BellPin stores the following locally on your device only, using Android's private app storage:

  • Subjects, timetable slots, and attendance records you add
  • Homework items (title, description, due date, priority, completion status)
  • Notes — both typed text notes and hand-drawn pad-style notes
  • Lecture audio recordings (AAC/M4A files) and any bookmarks you pin during recording
  • Blackboard/whiteboard photos and the PDFs generated from them (Scan tool)
  • Documents you add under the Scan tool's Documents category, and an optional profile photo (Settings)
  • App preferences (theme, attendance target percentage, PIN lock state)

None of this data is transmitted to MTP CODE, to any analytics service, or to any third party, unless you explicitly enable Google Drive Backup (Section 3). There is no cloud sync by default — the only other backup mechanism is the in-app "Backup & Restore" feature, which writes a file only to a location you choose on your own device or storage.

3. Optional Google Drive Backup (BellPin Pro)

If you purchase BellPin Pro and choose to sign in with your Google account and turn on Backup, BellPin uploads a copy of your app data to a hidden, app-only folder in your own Google Drive account (Drive's "appdata" space — a private folder that only BellPin can access, not visible in the regular Drive app). This is used solely to restore your data if you reinstall the app or switch devices. MTP CODE never receives a copy of this data — it is stored within your own Google account and transmitted directly between your device and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

To stop this and delete the backup, either turn off Backup in BellPin's Settings, or revoke BellPin's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also deletes the app-only Drive folder.

4. What we do not collect

  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers are read or shared
  • No sale of any data, and no sharing beyond the optional Google Drive Backup described above

5. Permissions BellPin requests, and why

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only used while you actively start a lecture recording from within the app. Audio is written straight to a file on your device; BellPin does not stream or upload microphone audio anywhere.
  • Camera — only used when you tap "Scan" to photograph a blackboard/whiteboard, which is then converted to a local PDF. BellPin uses the system camera app for this and does not access the camera in the background.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used to show the ongoing "recording in progress" notification and period/homework reminder alerts. No notification content is sent off-device.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — used so period-transition and homework-due reminders fire on time; this only schedules local alarms and does not involve any network access.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — used to re-schedule your timetable alerts after your device restarts, since Android clears scheduled alarms on reboot.

6. BellPin Pro purchase and PIN lock

The optional one-time "BellPin Pro" unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — that is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. Pro also unlocks an optional app-open PIN lock; the PIN you set is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere, including in Drive backups.

7. Sharing and export

If you use BellPin's Share button (for a lecture recording, scan, or note), the file is handed to the destination app you pick (for example WhatsApp, Drive, or email) through Android's standard share sheet. That destination app's own privacy policy applies once the file leaves BellPin. If you use "Backup & Restore," the backup file is written only to the storage location you select — BellPin does not upload it anywhere unless you separately enable Google Drive Backup.

8. Recycle Bin

Deleted subjects, homework, notes, recordings, and scans are moved to an in-app Recycle Bin rather than deleted immediately, so you can restore them if removed by mistake. They remain on your device, in the same private app storage, until you permanently delete them from the Recycle Bin.

9. Children's privacy

BellPin is a study utility that may be used by students of any age, including under 13. The core app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Google Sign-In and Drive Backup are optional Pro features intended for use by the account holder; if a minor's device is signed into a parent's or their own Google account, the same Google account terms and this policy's Section 3 apply.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date above will change when material updates are made.
