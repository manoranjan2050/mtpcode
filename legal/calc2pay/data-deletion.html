Calc2Pay Data Deletion

Calc2Pay · Last updated August 7, 2026

We believe you should always be able to remove your data. Here's how, depending on how you use Calc2Pay.

Deleting App Data

Because Calc2Pay stores its data locally on your device, you can delete everything at once by uninstalling the app, or clear just the app's data via Android Settings → Apps → Calc2Pay → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Advertising Identifier

Calc2Pay does not share an advertising identifier with any third party.

Contact

For any other data deletion request, email electroiot.in@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.
