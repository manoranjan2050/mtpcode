Calc2Pay Privacy Policy

Calc2Pay · Last updated August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manoranjan Das ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Calc2Pay, an Android app published under the MTPCode brand.

Information We Collect

Calc2Pay does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on our servers. All bills, items and settings are saved locally on your device only.

Permissions

Calc2Pay requests no special device permissions beyond local storage access to save your bill history on your device. We request only what is necessary for the app's core features to work.

Third-Party Services

Calc2Pay does not integrate any advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data Security

Because most data stays on your device (or your own infrastructure, for device-local storage), the largest security factor is the security of your own device or server. We recommend keeping your device or server updated and access-controlled.

Children's Privacy

Calc2Pay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
