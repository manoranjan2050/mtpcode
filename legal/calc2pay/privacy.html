Calc2Pay Privacy Policy

Calc2Pay · Last updated August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP Code (Manoranjan), ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Calc2Pay, a billing calculator and UPI payment QR generator for Android.

What We Store

Calc2Pay stores your shop profiles, products, and bills entirely on your device using local storage (Hive). This data is never uploaded to MTP Code's servers or any third party — the app works fully offline.

Payment QR Codes

Calc2Pay does not process payments. Payment QR codes are generated locally from the Payment ID (UPI ID) you enter in Shop Profile. Scanning the QR opens the customer's own UPI app to complete payment directly with your bank — Calc2Pay is never part of that transaction and never sees payment confirmations.

Permissions

Camera access is used only for scanning barcodes/QR codes, on demand, and is never recorded or stored. Microphone access is used only for the optional Voice Calculator and Voice Product Entry features, on demand, and audio is processed by your device's speech recognizer — we do not store audio recordings. Bluetooth access is used only to pair with and print to your own thermal receipt printer.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage data (including advertising identifiers) to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's own privacy policy. No other feature in the app shares data with third parties.

Backup Files You Create

The Backup feature in Settings exports your data to a JSON file you choose to save or share yourself. Calc2Pay does not upload this file anywhere automatically.

Cloud Backup (Google Drive)

Cloud Backup is an optional, opt-in Pro feature: nothing is uploaded unless you sign in with Google and tap Backup Now. Signing in uses Google Sign-In and requests only the narrow "drive.file" permission, meaning Calc2Pay can only see files it created itself — never your other Drive files or folders. Your backup is stored in your own Google Drive account, governed by Google's own privacy policy; we never see or store a copy of it on any server.

Calc2Pay Pro Purchases

Upgrading to Calc2Pay Pro is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google processes the purchase and tells the app whether it succeeded, governed by Google Play's own privacy policy.

Data Security

Because your data stays on your own device, the largest security factor is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device updated and access-controlled (screen lock, etc.).

Children's Privacy

Calc2Pay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
