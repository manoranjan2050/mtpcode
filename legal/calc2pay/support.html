Need help with Calc2Pay? Here's the fastest way to reach us.

Email Support

Write to electroiot.in@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your Calc2Pay version, and your device/OS. We aim to respond within 2-3 business days.

Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If Calc2Pay has a public GitHub repository, you're welcome to open an issue there with reproduction steps. Otherwise, email us directly.

Before You Contact Us

  • Make sure you're on the latest version — check the app page for the current release and changelog.
  • For unexpected results, try restarting the app once.
