Denomiq Data Deletion

Denomiq – Cash Counter · Last updated August 10, 2026

Denomiq stores cash count data on your device only. MTP CODE does not host user cash records on a central server.

How to delete your data

  • Delete sessions in-app — use History to delete individual sessions.
  • Uninstall the app — clearing/uninstalling Denomiq removes local app storage on that device.
  • Exported files — files you saved or shared (PDF, CSV, JSON, Excel, etc.) must be deleted manually from those locations.
