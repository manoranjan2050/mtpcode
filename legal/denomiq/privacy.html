Denomiq Privacy Policy

Denomiq – Cash Counter · Last updated August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP CODE ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Denomiq – Cash Counter (package in.mtpcode.denomiq), an Android cash denomination counting utility.

Website: https://mtpcode.com
Support: mtpcode.in@gmail.com

1. Overview

Denomiq is offline-first. Core cash counting, history, reports, and exports work without an account and without uploading your cash records to MTP CODE servers.

2. Data stored on your device

Denomiq may store the following locally on your device:

  • Cash count sessions (note/coin quantities, totals, expected amount, difference/status)
  • Optional session details (store, counter, cashier, notes, opening/closing type)
  • Presets and preferences (stores/counters, denomination visibility, theme, keypad mode)
  • Exported files you choose to save or share

3. What we do not collect

  • No login or user account required
  • No SMS or phone-number verification
  • No upload of cash count records to MTP CODE servers
  • No sale of your cash-count data

4. Sharing and export

If you export, share, print, copy, or send a report (PDF, CSV, TXT, JSON, Excel), the content leaves the app only through the destination you choose (for example WhatsApp, Drive, email, Bluetooth, Thermal Desk, or a printer). Those third-party apps have their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising

The free version may show non-intrusive Google AdMob banner ads on Home, History, and Reports. Ads are not placed over cash-count controls or totals. AdMob may use advertising identifiers according to Google’s policies. Cash count contents are not sent to AdMob by Denomiq.

6. Purchases (Denomiq Pro)

Optional Pro upgrades are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google, not by MTP CODE. Pro may remove ads and unlock features such as Excel export.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — ads and optional purchase verification
  • Storage / file access via system pickers or share sheet — when you save, share, or restore backups

8. Children’s privacy

Denomiq is a business utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Disclaimer

Denomiq is a cash counting helper. It is not accounting, tax, banking, or legal software and does not provide financial advice.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when material updates are made.
