DocZest Cookie Policy

DocZest · Last updated August 8, 2026

DocZest is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. Document history and settings are stored using Android's local app storage, not cookies, and never leave your device.

Advertising SDK Identifiers

DocZest shows banner ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may use a device advertising identifier — a mobile-app equivalent of a cookie — to serve and measure ads, according to Google's own privacy policy. This is handled entirely by the AdMob SDK and is not something DocZest's own code reads or stores.

Managing Ad Personalization

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by Android version).
