DocZest Data Deletion

DocZest · Last updated August 8, 2026

We believe you should always be able to remove your data. Here's how, depending on how you use DocZest.

Deleting App Data

Because DocZest stores its documents, history and settings locally on your device only — there is no DocZest account and no DocZest server — you can delete everything at once by uninstalling the app, or clear just the app's data via Android Settings → Apps → DocZest → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Advertising Identifier

DocZest itself does not store or share an advertising identifier. The embedded Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK may use one to serve and measure ads — you can reset or opt out of it via your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Contact

For any other data deletion request, email mtpcode.in@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.
