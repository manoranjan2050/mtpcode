DocZest Privacy Policy

DocZest · Last updated August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manoranjan Das ("we", "us") handles information in connection with DocZest, an Android app published under the MTPCode brand.

Local Document Processing

All document and image operations (creating, compressing, converting, merging, splitting and scanning) run entirely on your device. Your documents, images and PDFs are never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any servers that receive them.

No Accounts

DocZest does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal identifiers to use its core features.

Local Storage Only

Settings and document history (filenames, sizes, dates) are stored only on your device using local storage. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this information.

Permissions

DocZest requests only the permissions needed for the feature you are using — for example, camera access only when scanning a document. You can deny any permission and the app will explain what that disables.

Advertising

DocZest shows banner ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, according to their own privacy policies. This data collection is limited to the advertising SDK and is not related to your documents.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your documents or personal data with third parties, other than the advertising data described above.

Children's Privacy

DocZest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mtpcode.in@gmail.com.
