DotGlow Cookie Policy

DotGlow: LED Matrix Clock · Package com.electroiot.dotglow · Effective 3 October 2026

DotGlow is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. The few preferences it keeps (app lock, cached setup-guide and About links, downloaded firmware cache) are stored in Android's local app storage on your device, not in cookies.

No advertising identifiers

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not use your device's advertising identifier.

The clock's web page

If you set a password on your DotGlow clock's local web settings page, the clock sets one session cookie in your browser to keep you logged in. It stays on your local network and is used for nothing else.
