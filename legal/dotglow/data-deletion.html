DotGlow Data Deletion

DotGlow: LED Matrix Clock · Package com.electroiot.dotglow · Effective 3 October 2026

DotGlow does not upload your data to our servers — there is no server and no account. Here's how to remove data kept on your phone and on your clock.

On your phone

Uninstall the app, or clear its data via Android Settings → Apps → DotGlow → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This removes the app lock, cached links and downloaded firmware. To remove a clock pairing, open Android Settings → Bluetooth, tap the DotGlow clock and choose Forget.

On your clock

In the app use System → Factory reset to erase the clock's settings, including its WiFi name and password and its paired phones. Without the app, hold the clock's BOOT button for 5 seconds to forget its WiFi.
