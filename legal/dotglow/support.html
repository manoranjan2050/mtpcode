DotGlow Support

DotGlow: LED Matrix Clock · Package com.electroiot.dotglow · Effective 3 October 2026

Need help with DotGlow? Here's the fastest way to reach us.

Email support

Write to admin@mtpcode.com with a description of the issue, your app version, your board (ESP32 / ESP32-S3) and your phone model. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Before you contact us

  • Display dim, all LEDs stuck on, or the board keeps restarting? Power the clock from a 5 V / 1 A (or stronger) phone charger — a computer USB port is too weak. Add a 470–1000 µF capacitor across the display's VCC and GND.
  • Can't pair? Type the 6-digit PIN shown on the clock's LEDs (not 0000 or 1234) within about 30 seconds.
  • ESP32 DevKit won't start after wiring? No wire may be on the pins labelled CLK, SD0–SD3 or CMD. The display's CLK goes to D18.
  • Display upside down? Use the rotate button on the dashboard, or double-press the clock's BOOT button.
  • The in-app Setup guide has wiring pictures, power tips and a full troubleshooting list. No clock yet? Tap Try the demo clock.
