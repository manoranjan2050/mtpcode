Geo Camera & GPS Stamp Cookie Policy

Geo Camera & GPS Stamp · Last updated 13 August 2026

Geo Camera & GPS Stamp is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. Preferences it keeps (theme, stamp fields, onboarding, and similar settings) are stored using Android’s local app storage, not cookies, and stay on your device.

Advertising identifiers

The free version may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob does not use browser cookies inside the app, but it may use your device’s advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, governed by Google’s privacy policy.
