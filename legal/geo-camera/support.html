Need help with Geo Camera? Here’s the fastest way to reach us.

Email support

Write to mtpcode.in@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your app version, and your device/OS. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Before you contact us

  • Make sure you’re on the latest version — check the app page for the current release.
  • If GPS is missing, confirm Location is allowed and the device has a real fix. The app will not invent coordinates.
  • If the compass says unavailable, hold the phone away from metal and closer to vertical.
