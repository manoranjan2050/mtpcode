IDPhotoCraft Cookie Policy

IDPhotoCraft · Last updated August 9, 2026

IDPhotoCraft is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. Preferences it keeps (theme, DPI, onboarding, and similar settings) are stored using Android’s local app storage, not cookies, and stay on your device.

Advertising identifiers

The free version may show ads via Google AdMob. AdMob does not use browser cookies inside the app, but it may use your device’s advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, governed by Google’s privacy policy.
