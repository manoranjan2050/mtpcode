IDPhotoCraft Data Deletion

IDPhotoCraft · Last updated August 9, 2026

IDPhotoCraft does not upload your photos to our servers. Here’s how to remove data that stays on your device or is handled by Google.

Deleting app data

Clear everything at once by uninstalling the app, or clear just the app’s data via Android Settings → Apps → IDPhotoCraft → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Exported images you saved to your gallery remain under your control — delete those files yourself if needed.

Purchases

Pro purchases are managed by Google Play. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Play Store. We do not store a separate copy of your payment details.

Advertising identifier

You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device’s system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording may vary by OEM).

Contact

For any other deletion request, email mtpcode.in@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.
