IDPhotoCraft Privacy Policy

IDPhotoCraft · Package com.idphotocraft.idphotocraft · Last updated August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IDPhotoCraft (“we”, “the app”), published by MTP Code (Manoranjan Das), handles information. IDPhotoCraft helps you create ID, passport, and visa-style photos on your Android device.

Photos and on-device processing

Photos you capture or select are processed locally on your device for face detection, editing, background replacement, formatting, export, and print sheets. IDPhotoCraft does not upload your photos to our servers. We do not operate a photo-storage cloud for this app, and the app itself does not transmit your images to us.

Data stored on your device

The app may store preferences (theme, DPI, paper size, onboarding completion) and recent project references using on-device storage (SharedPreferences / local files). You can clear this by clearing the app’s data in Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take a new photo for ID processing.
  • Photos / media — to choose an existing image from your gallery.
  • Internet — to download on-device ML models via Google Play Services when needed, to load ads (AdMob), and to process Google Play Billing purchases.

Camera and photos access are used only for the features you initiate. We do not use these permissions to upload or share your photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may show ads served by Google AdMob. The advertising SDK may collect device and usage information (such as advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data) according to Google’s policies. In regions where required, a consent form (UMP) may be shown before personalized ads.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

Pro purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional Pro unlocks (yearly subscription pro_yearly and lifetime purchase pro_lifetime) are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details — Google processes the purchase and tells the app whether it succeeded, governed by Google Play’s privacy policy. A legacy product ID remove_ads may still be recognized if previously purchased.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). Do not use the app to create photos of children unless you are their parent or legal guardian with authority to do so. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data security

Because photos and preferences stay on your device, the largest security factor is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device updated and access-controlled (screen lock, etc.).

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@idphotocraft.app.
