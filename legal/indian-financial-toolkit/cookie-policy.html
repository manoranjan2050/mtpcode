Indian Financial Toolkit Cookie Policy

Indian Financial Toolkit · Last updated August 20, 2026

Indian Financial Toolkit is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. Any data it keeps — saved calculations, favourites, or your theme preference — is stored using Android's local app storage, not cookies, and never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

Embedded Web Content

Indian Financial Toolkit does not embed third-party web views that would set cookies on your behalf.
