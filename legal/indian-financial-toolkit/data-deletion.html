Indian Financial Toolkit Data Deletion

Indian Financial Toolkit · Last updated August 20, 2026

We believe you should always be able to remove your data. Here's how, for Indian Financial Toolkit.

Deleting App Data

Because Indian Financial Toolkit stores everything locally on your device, you have two ways to remove it:

  • Inside the app: go to Settings → History and tap "Clear all" to remove every saved calculation, or unstar items from Favorites.
  • At the OS level: uninstall the app, or clear its data via Android Settings → Apps → Indian Financial Toolkit → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

No Server-Side Data

Since Indian Financial Toolkit never uploads your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end — deleting it on your device is complete and final.
