Indian Financial Toolkit Privacy Policy

Indian Financial Toolkit · Last updated August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP Code (MANORANJAN) ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Indian Financial Toolkit, an Android app published under the MTPCode brand.

Information We Collect

Indian Financial Toolkit does not collect or transmit any information you enter. Every calculation — EMI, SIP, tax, retirement, or otherwise — runs entirely on your device. We do not operate a server, and the app has no network permission for sending your data anywhere.

Data Stored On Your Device

If you choose to save a calculation to History or mark a calculator as a favourite, that information is stored locally on your device only, using Android's local app storage. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared unless you explicitly use the app's own Backup export feature to share a file through Android's native share sheet — a file you choose the destination for.

Permissions

Indian Financial Toolkit requests no special device permissions — no camera, location, contacts, SMS, or broad storage access. It works fully in airplane mode.

Third-Party Services

Indian Financial Toolkit does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDK that collects data. It does not use Google AdMob or any other ad network.

Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, the primary security factor is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device's OS updated and using a screen lock.

Children's Privacy

Indian Financial Toolkit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children — indeed, we do not collect data from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mtpcode.in@gmail.com.
