Kalinga Raktabir Data Deletion

Kalinga Raktabir – Blood donation camps, alerts & donor certificates · Last updated November 25, 2026

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Kalinga Raktabir does not have user accounts. Data in the app is of two kinds:

1. On your phone: language, theme and cached camps. Uninstall the app or use Android Settings > Apps > Kalinga Raktabir > Storage > Clear data to remove it.

2. Your donor record held by the Federation (name, blood group, donations). To have it corrected or deleted, or to stop certificate lookup for your number:

- Email mtpcode.in@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my donor record" and the mobile number registered at the camp, or

- Tell any volunteer at a blood donation camp.

We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days. Security logs (IP address and a hashed number) are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Questions: mtpcode.in@gmail.com
