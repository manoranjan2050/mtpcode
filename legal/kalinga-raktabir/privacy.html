Kalinga Raktabir Privacy Policy

Kalinga Raktabir – Blood donation camps, alerts & donor certificates · Last updated November 25, 2026

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Kalinga Raktabir is the public app of Kalinga Humanitarian Aid Youth Federation, Balikuda, Odisha, India (the "Federation"). It shows blood donation camps and notices, and lets a donor fetch their own donation certificates.

1. What we handle

  • Mobile number you type in "Get my certificate". It is sent over HTTPS to our server only to find your donation record. The app does not store it.
  • Certificate details returned for that number: name, blood group, gender, number of donations, donation dates, camp names and certificate numbers. We never send your phone number, address or age to the app. To see certificates you must enter your mobile number and also your certificate number or your last donation date. Your camp photo is sent only when you enter your certificate number, and the app keeps it on your phone for your certificate.
  • Security log kept by the server: your IP address and an irreversible scrambled (hashed) form of the number you searched. It exists only to stop people from guessing numbers, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • On your phone: the photo you may choose to put on your own certificate (it never leaves your phone), language, colour theme, notification choice, the last camps and notices (so they show offline) and the list of items you were already told about.

2. What we do not collect

Your location, contacts, photos, files, advertising ID or device identifiers. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app checks our server about once an hour for new camps and notices and shows an alert on your phone. Nothing about you is sent besides a normal request for the public camp list. You can turn alerts off with the bell icon or in Android settings.

4. Who can see your certificates

Anyone who knows your registered mobile number and solves the on-screen check can see your name, blood group and donation count. If you do not want this, ask the Federation to remove your record (see section 7).

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. Certificates are shared only when you choose to share them (for example on WhatsApp). Our database is hosted by Supabase (region: Mumbai, India) which processes data only on our behalf.

6. Security

All requests use HTTPS. Donor records can only be read by signed-in Federation volunteers or through the limited lookup described above, which limits repeated attempts.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You may ask us to correct or delete your donor record, or to stop certificate lookup for your number, at any time. Write to mtpcode.in@gmail.com from the same details you gave at the camp, or tell any camp volunteer. We will act within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

8. Children

Blood donors must be adults (18 years or older). The app is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change. Continued use means you accept the update.
