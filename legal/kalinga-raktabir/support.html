Need help with Kalinga Raktabir?

Contact

Email mtpcode.in@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • I cannot find my certificate. Check that you typed the mobile number you gave at the camp. If it still fails, ask a camp volunteer to check the number in their records.
  • I did not get alerts. Open the app and tap the bell icon until it says "Alerts on", and allow notifications for the app in Android settings. Alerts arrive about once an hour.
  • How do I change the language? Tap the language button at the top right of the home screen.
  • How do I remove my record? See the Data Deletion page.
