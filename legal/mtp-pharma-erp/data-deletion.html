MTP Pharma ERP Data Deletion

MTP Pharma ERP · Last updated August 7, 2026

We believe you should always be able to remove your data. Here's how, depending on how you use MTP Pharma ERP.

Deleting Your Business Data

Since your pharmacy's billing, inventory and supplier data lives in a database you control, you (or your system administrator) can delete records directly from within MTP Pharma ERP, or drop/reset the database entirely if you are decommissioning the installation.

Deleting Your License Record

To remove the license and device-activation record associated with your installation from the Kavach license server, email mtpcode.in@gmail.com with your license key and we will delete it within 14 days.

Contact

For any other data deletion request, email mtpcode.in@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.
