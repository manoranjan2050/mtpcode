MTP Pharma ERP Privacy Policy

MTP Pharma ERP · Last updated August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manoranjan Das ("we", "us") handles information in connection with MTP Pharma ERP, a web and desktop application published under the MTPCode brand.

Information We Collect

MTP Pharma ERP stores your pharmacy’s billing, inventory and supplier data in a database that you control — either on your own server (web build) or locally via the bundled MariaDB instance (desktop build). We do not access, copy or transmit your business data to MTPCode servers. The only data shared with us is license validation traffic (a license key and device identifier) sent to the Kavach license server to verify your subscription.

Permissions

MTP Pharma ERP requests standard web/desktop application permissions for your operating system (local database access for the desktop build). We request only what is necessary for the app's core features to work.

Third-Party Services

MTP Pharma ERP does not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. License validation communicates with our own Kavach license server.

Data Security

Because most data stays on your device (or your own infrastructure, for the ERP’s database), the largest security factor is the security of your own device or server. We recommend keeping your device or server updated and access-controlled.

Children's Privacy

MTP Pharma ERP is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
