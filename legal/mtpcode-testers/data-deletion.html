MTPCode Testers Data Deletion

MTPCode Testers · Last updated August 20, 2026

You can request deletion of your MTPCode Testers account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Email mtpcode.in@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will verify the request and delete your account within 14 days.

What Gets Deleted

Your profile, devices, app submissions, campaigns, feedback, bug reports, credit and reputation history, and notification data are deleted. Records required for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes (e.g. admin action logs relating to abuse investigations) may be retained as permitted by law.

Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling the MTPCode Testers app removes the app and any local cache from your device, but does not delete your account or server-side data — use the email request above for that.

Contact

For any data deletion request, email mtpcode.in@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.
