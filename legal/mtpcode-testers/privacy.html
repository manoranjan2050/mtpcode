MTPCode Testers Privacy Policy

MTPCode Testers · Last updated August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP Code (MANORANJAN) ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in connection with MTPCode Testers — an Android app and web platform that connects developers with testers for real, structured Android app testing.

Information We Collect

MTPCode Testers requires an account. We collect only what's needed to run the platform:

  • Account information — name, email address, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), role (developer or tester), and optionally your country.
  • Device information (testers) — manufacturer, model, Android version, architecture, screen size, RAM range and declared hardware capabilities (e.g. NFC, camera, biometric) that you choose to add to your tester profile, used only to match you to compatible testing campaigns. We do not collect persistent hardware identifiers (e.g. IMEI, Android ID) for this purpose.
  • App and campaign data (developers) — the app details, versions, screenshots and testing requirements you submit, and the campaigns you create.
  • Testing activity — task completions, testing session start/end times, structured feedback ratings and comments, and bug reports (including any screenshots or videos you choose to attach).
  • Platform activity — credit transactions, reputation score, badges earned, and notification preferences.
  • Push notification token — if you enable notifications, a device token used to deliver them via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We do not secretly monitor your device or activity outside the app, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to operate the platform: authenticating you, matching testers to compatible campaigns, showing developers structured feedback and bug reports, calculating credits and reputation, and sending notifications you've opted into (new assignments, campaign updates, feedback/bug updates, credits earned).

Sharing With Other Users

A developer can see the testers assigned to their campaign (name, device/testing activity relevant to that campaign) and their feedback/bug reports. A tester can see the app details and instructions for campaigns they're matched to. We do not expose your email address or password to other users.

Third-Party Services

MTPCode Testers uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to deliver push notifications, and Google Play Billing to process optional Pro subscription purchases — Google processes payment details directly; we never see or store your payment card information. Purchase verification uses a purchase token only.

Data Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. API access uses per-device authentication tokens. All traffic to our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). We recommend keeping your device updated and access-controlled (e.g. a screen lock).

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. See our Data Deletion page for how to request removal of your account and associated data.

Children's Privacy

MTPCode Testers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
