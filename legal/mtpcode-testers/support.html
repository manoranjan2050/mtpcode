MTPCode Testers Support

MTPCode Testers · Last updated August 20, 2026

Need help with MTPCode Testers? Here's the fastest way to reach us.

Email Support

Write to mtpcode.in@gmail.com with a description of the issue, whether you're a developer or tester, and your device/OS if relevant. We aim to respond within 2-3 business days.

Common Questions

  • How does tester matching work? Testers are matched to campaigns automatically based on their device, Android version and declared hardware capabilities against a campaign's requirements — no manual "joining" is needed.
  • What are credits for? Credits are earned through genuine testing activity (completing tasks, submitting feedback, confirmed bug reports) and can be used by developers toward testing campaigns.
  • Does MTPCode Testers guarantee my app gets approved on Google Play? No — MTPCode Testers supports legitimate closed testing and structured feedback, but does not guarantee any Play Store outcome.

Report Abuse

If you encounter spam, fraud, fake testing activity or harassment, use the in-app report option or email us directly with details.
