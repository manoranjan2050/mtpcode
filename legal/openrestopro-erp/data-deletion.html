OpenRestoPro ERP Data Deletion

OpenRestoPro ERP – Billing POS · Package in.mtpcode.restoproerp · Last updated 5 November 2026

OpenRestoPro ERP stores your data on the OpenRestoPro ERP server you sign in to, not inside the app. Here is how to remove it.

Data on your phone

  • Open the app → More → Settings & account → Sign out. This removes the sign-in token and saved details from the phone.
  • Or uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything it stored on the phone (including printer settings).

Your login account

Staff logins (manager, cashier) are created by the shop owner. Ask the owner to delete your user in the OpenRestoPro ERP website. The owner can delete staff, customers, bills, expenses and other records from the website at any time.

Server hosted by MTP Code

If MTP Code hosts the OpenRestoPro ERP server for your business, the owner can ask us to delete the account and all of its data by emailing admin@mtpcode.com from the owner's registered contact, with the website address. We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days. Backups that the owner saved to their own Dropbox or Google Drive are controlled by the owner and must be deleted there.
