OpenRestoPro ERP Privacy Policy

OpenRestoPro ERP – Billing POS · Package in.mtpcode.restoproerp · Effective 5 November 2026 (updated for ads and Pro)

This policy is for the Android app OpenRestoPro ERP – Billing POS (in.mtpcode.restoproerp), published by MTP Code / Manoranjan. Contact: admin@mtpcode.com.

The app is the mobile client for the open-source OpenRestoPro ERP website, a billing and back-office system for restaurants, cake shops, cafés and hotels. The app itself does not have its own cloud: it connects only to the OpenRestoPro ERP server address you enter when you sign in (a server run by your business, or one hosted for your business). The free version shows a Google AdMob banner ad; buying Pro (a one-time Google Play purchase) removes ads. The app has no third-party analytics, and we do not sell personal data.

Who controls your data

Everything you enter in the app is saved on the OpenRestoPro ERP server you sign in to. The business that runs that server (usually the shop owner) controls that data. If MTP Code hosts a server for your business, we store it only to provide the service to that business and do not use it for anything else.

What data the app handles

  • Sign-in: the server address, your username and password. The password is sent to your server over HTTPS to sign in; the app keeps only a sign-in token and your name and role on the phone, not the password.
  • Business records you enter: bills and orders, items and prices, daily collections, expenses, vendors and payments, stock, reports.
  • Customer details you choose to add to a bill or order, such as a name, mobile number and delivery address.
  • Staff details entered by the owner or manager, such as names, roles, attendance, salary and payments.
  • Bill photos: if you attach a photo to an expense, the photo you take or pick is resized and uploaded to your server. The app opens the camera or photo picker only when you tap it.
  • Printer settings: the Bluetooth thermal printer you choose and paper width are stored on the phone only.
  • Ads (free version only): Google AdMob, part of Google, shows the banner. To serve and measure ads, AdMob can collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location from it), app and device information, and ad interactions. See how Google uses data from partner apps. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Pro purchase: payment is handled by Google Play. The app only receives a confirmation that your Google account owns Pro; we never see your card or UPI details.
  • Not collected by the app: precise location, contacts, call logs, crash reports or usage analytics.

Permissions and why

  • Internet: to talk to your OpenRestoPro ERP server.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: only to connect to a paired Bluetooth receipt or kitchen printer and print. It is not used to find your location.
  • Camera and photos are used through Android's own camera and photo picker, only when you add a bill photo.

Sharing

  • Business data is sent only to the server you sign in to, over HTTPS.
  • In the free version, ad-related data described above is shared with Google AdMob to show ads. Pro users do not load ads.
  • When you tap Share or Send on WhatsApp, the bill (PDF or link) goes to the app or person you choose.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share your business records with advertisers or data brokers.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords on the server are stored hashed. Owner, manager and cashier roles limit what each person can see; cashiers only see the counter.

Keeping and deleting data

Records stay on your server until the business deletes them. Signing out removes the sign-in token and saved details from the phone, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone. To delete data held on a server, see Data deletion.

Children

The app is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
