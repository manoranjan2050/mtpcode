Raktabir Admin Data Deletion

Raktabir Admin – Donor records, certificates & emergency finder for blood camps · Last updated November 25, 2026

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Raktabir Admin stores donor records on the volunteer's phone and, when cloud sync is on, in the Federation's database.

  • To remove one donor or one donation: open the donor in the app and use Delete. The change syncs to the cloud.
  • To remove all data from a phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • A donor who wants their record corrected or deleted can email mtpcode.in@gmail.com with the mobile number registered at the camp, or tell any camp volunteer. We complete requests within 30 days.

Questions: mtpcode.in@gmail.com
