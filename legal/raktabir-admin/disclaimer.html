Raktabir Admin Disclaimer

Raktabir Admin – Donor records, certificates & emergency finder for blood camps · Last updated November 25, 2026

Raktabir Admin is an information and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice.

  • Eligibility dates are only reminders. A doctor at the camp decides whether a person can donate.
  • Emergency finder results show people who may be able to donate. Always confirm with the donor and the hospital.
  • In a medical emergency call 108 or go to the nearest hospital.
