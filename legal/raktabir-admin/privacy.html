Raktabir Admin Privacy Policy

Raktabir Admin – Donor records, certificates & emergency finder for blood camps · Last updated November 25, 2026

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Raktabir Admin is the volunteer tool of Kalinga Humanitarian Aid Youth Federation, Balikuda, Odisha (the "Federation"). It is used at blood donation camps to record donors, issue certificates and post camps and notices. It is not meant for the general public.

1. What the app handles

  • Donor details entered by a volunteer, with the donor's consent: name, mobile number, blood group, age, gender, address or village, photograph, camp name and donation dates.
  • The volunteer's own sign-in (email address) when cloud sync is turned on.
  • A secret key used to sign certificate QR codes.
  • Posts made by the Federation: camps, notices and community links. These are public by design.

2. Where the data is stored

  • On the phone: a local database and photo folder. The app works fully offline.
  • If a volunteer signs in under Settings > Cloud sync, records and photos are uploaded over HTTPS to the Federation's database (Supabase, region Mumbai, India) so that volunteers' phones share one list. Only signed-in volunteers can read or change donor records.
  • Donors can look up their own certificates in the public donor app. That lookup shows only name, blood group, gender, donation count, dates, camp names and certificate numbers, and limits repeated attempts.

3. How the data is used

  • To create certificates and share images for donors
  • To find donors of a needed blood group in an emergency (call or WhatsApp)
  • To remind donors when they can donate again
  • To verify certificates by scanning their QR code

4. What is not collected

Location, contacts, advertising ID or analytics. The app contains no advertising and no third-party trackers.

5. Sharing and backups

Certificates are shared only when a volunteer chooses to share them. A backup is a file that the volunteer saves or sends; anyone with that file and the app can read the records, so volunteers must keep it private. The Federation does not sell or rent data.

6. Security

Optional PIN lock on the app, HTTPS for all cloud traffic, and access rules that allow only signed-in volunteers to read donor records. Volunteers must keep their phone and login private.

7. Retention and deletion

Records are kept until a volunteer deletes them in the app; deletions also sync to the cloud. A donor may ask for correction or deletion at any time (see the Data Deletion page).

8. Children

Donors must be adults (18 years or older).
