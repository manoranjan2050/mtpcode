Resto Pro Data Deletion

Resto Pro · Last updated 10 September 2026

Resto Pro stores your account and business data in the cloud (Supabase) so it can sync across devices. Here's how to remove it.

Local, on-device data

Your App PIN, theme preference, and current session are stored only on your device. Clear these by uninstalling the app, or via Android Settings → Apps → Resto Pro → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This does not delete your cloud account or business data.

Deleting your account and cloud data

The app does not yet have a self-serve "Delete Account" button. To request full deletion of your account and every record tied to it (cash entries, expenses, vendors, staff records, and ledger history), email admin@mtpcode.com from the email address your account is registered under, with the subject "Resto Pro — Delete my account". We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and data within 14 days, and confirm by email once done. This action cannot be undone — export a backup first via Settings → Backup & Restore if you want to keep a copy.

Individual records

You can delete individual entries (a collection entry, an expense, an employee, etc.) yourself at any time directly in the app, without needing to delete your whole account.

Contact

For any other deletion request, email admin@mtpcode.com and we will respond within 14 days.
