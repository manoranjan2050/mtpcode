Resto Pro Support

Resto Pro · Package in.mtpcode.restopro · Last updated 10 September 2026

Need help with Resto Pro? Here's the fastest way to reach us.

Email support

Write to admin@mtpcode.com (or mtpcode.in@gmail.com) with a description of the issue, your app version, and your device/OS. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Before you contact us

  • Make sure you're on the latest version — check the app page for the current release.
  • Data missing or looks empty after reinstalling or switching phones? Make sure you're logged into the same account (Settings → Account & Sync shows the email you're signed in as) — Log In with your existing email/password rather than creating a new account.
  • Forgot your App PIN? Clear the app's local storage (Android Settings → Apps → Resto Pro → Storage → Clear storage) — this only resets the on-device PIN/theme, your cloud account and business data are untouched.
  • The app needs an internet connection to load or save anything — it's cloud-synced by design, not offline-first.
