Smart SIP Calculator Privacy Policy

Smart SIP Calculator · Last updated August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP Code (MANORANJAN) ("we", "us") handles information in connection with Smart SIP Calculator, an Android app published under the MTPCode brand.

Information We Collect

Smart SIP Calculator does not collect or transmit the figures you enter. All calculations happen on your device and nothing is saved beyond your current session unless you choose to share a result.

Permissions

Smart SIP Calculator requests no special device permissions. We request only what is necessary for the app's core features to work.

Third-Party Services

Smart SIP Calculator uses Google AdMob to show ads and may share a device advertising identifier with Google for this purpose. See Google’s privacy policy for details on how they process this data.

Data Security

Because most data stays on your device (or your own infrastructure, for device-local storage), the largest security factor is the security of your own device or server. We recommend keeping your device or server updated and access-controlled.

Children's Privacy

Smart SIP Calculator is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@mtpcode.com.
