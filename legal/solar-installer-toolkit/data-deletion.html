Solar Installer Toolkit Data Deletion

Solar Installer Toolkit · Last updated 30 August 2026

Solar Installer Toolkit does not upload your project data, photos, or GPS track to our servers — there is no server. Here's how to remove data that stays on your device.

Deleting app data

Clear everything at once by uninstalling the app, or clear just the app's data via Android Settings → Apps → Solar Installer Toolkit → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Backup files, reports, or photos you already shared or copied to another app remain under your control — delete those files yourself if needed.

Backup files

Backup files created via Settings → "Backup all data" are saved to the app's own storage on your device and are only shared further if you explicitly choose to share them. Delete a backup file the same way as any other file on your device, or clear app storage to remove all of them at once.

Contact

For any other deletion request, email admin@mtpcode.com and we will respond within 14 days.
