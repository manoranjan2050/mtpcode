Solar Installer Toolkit Privacy Policy

Solar Installer Toolkit · Package in.mtpcode.solartoolkit · Effective 31 August 2026

This policy is for the Android app Solar Installer Toolkit (in.mtpcode.solartoolkit), published by MTP Code / Manoranjan. Contact: admin@mtpcode.com or mtpcode.in@gmail.com. Website: mtpcode.com.

Solar Installer Toolkit is offline-first. All project data — customer details, site surveys, PV/inverter/battery specs, measurements, checklists, photos, notes, issues, and signatures — is stored only in a local database on your device. There is no account, no backend server, and we do not sell personal information.

What data the app uses

  • Project and customer details you enter (name, phone, email, site address, notes) — on device only, for your own project records.
  • Camera frames and saved JPEGs — on device, for photo evidence; originals are never overwritten by the optional GPS-stamped watermark copy.
  • GPS coordinates, altitude, accuracy — on device, captured only when you explicitly tap "Get current location" or take a photo with location tagging enabled.
  • Best-effort address lookup — when online, the app may resolve your GPS coordinates to a place name using Android's on-device geocoding service, purely to label the photo stamp. This is the app's only network call. If you're offline, the app falls back to your own project's site address, or shows coordinates only — capture is never blocked.
  • App PIN / biometric lock (optional) — a salted hash of the PIN is stored locally; biometric unlock uses Android's own BiometricPrompt and never leaves the device.
  • Backup files — created only when you tap "Backup all data"; the file is written to your device's storage and only leaves the device if you explicitly share it via the Android share sheet.
  • Diagnostics — not collected.

We do not operate a server that receives your project data, photos, or location.

Location

Location is used to tag site surveys and photos with GPS coordinates, for professional field documentation. It is requested just-in-time, only when you use a feature that needs it, after an in-app explanation. You can decline and continue using the rest of the app; features that need location will simply record no coordinates rather than inventing them.

Camera and photos

Photos are captured only when you use the app's camera features and are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded automatically.

Sharing

The app can generate PDF reports, CSV exports, and backup files, and hand them to Android's share sheet at your request. What happens after that point (which app you choose to share to) is outside this app's control and governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Third-party services

This app uses Android's on-device location services (Google Play Services Fused Location Provider) to read GPS coordinates when you request them, and Android's on-device Geocoder for the optional best-effort address lookup described above.

Advertising

The free version of this app shows banner ads (Home, Report, and Settings screens) served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID and other device/usage signals to serve and measure ads, per Google's Privacy Policy. No project data, photos, location, or other content you create in this app is ever shared with AdMob or any ad network. You can remove ads permanently with the one-time "Go Pro" in-app purchase.

Purchases

The "Go Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment details — only whether the purchase completed, which is recorded locally on your device to unlock the ad-free experience.

Accounts and children

No account is required. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, contact us and we will help you remove local data by uninstalling or clearing app storage.

Your choices

  • Deny camera or location in Android settings — the app still works, minus the features that need them.
  • Turn off individual watermark fields (including GPS) in Settings → Photo overlay.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage, to delete all local data.
