Solar Installer Toolkit · Package in.mtpcode.solartoolkit · Last updated 30 August 2026

Need help with Solar Installer Toolkit? Here's the fastest way to reach us.

Email support

Write to admin@mtpcode.com (or mtpcode.in@gmail.com) with a description of the issue, your app version, and your device/OS. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Before you contact us

  • Make sure you're on the latest version — check the app page for the current release.
  • If GPS is missing, confirm Location is allowed and the device has a real fix. The app will not invent coordinates.
  • Lost your PIN? Use Settings → App Lock → Remove App Lock after confirming with fingerprint/face, or clear app data as a last resort (this also clears your projects — back up first).
  • Back up regularly via Settings → "Backup all data" — project data lives only on your device.
