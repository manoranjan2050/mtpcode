Solvexa Data Deletion

Solvexa · Last updated 10 September 2026

Solvexa has no account and no backend server — there is nothing of yours stored on our systems to delete. Everything (calculator history, photos, any optional project/report notes, and preferences) stays on your device. Here's how to remove it.

Deleting app data

Clear everything at once by uninstalling the app, or clear just the app's data via Android Settings → Apps → Solvexa → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Photos, reports, or exports you already shared or copied to another app remain under your control — delete those files yourself if needed.

GPS Camera photos

GPS Camera photos are saved to the app's own storage on your device. Delete individual photos from within the app, or clear app storage to remove all of them at once.

Optional project/report data

If you've used the optional project/report feature (Settings → Project Reports), that data is stored the same way as everything else — locally, deleted the same way (per-item delete in the app, or a full clear via uninstall/clear storage).

Contact

For any other deletion request, email admin@mtpcode.com and we will respond within 14 days.
