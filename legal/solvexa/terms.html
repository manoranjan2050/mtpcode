The service

Solvexa is a tool-first field toolkit for solar installers and electricians: a dashboard of standalone tools (GPS Camera, Compass, Angle Meter, Level, and around 20 electrical/solar calculators) plus a Guides & Formulas reference. There is no account and no login — you open a tool and use it.
