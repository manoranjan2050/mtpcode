STOCKVEXA Data Deletion

STOCKVEXA – Inventory & Billing · Last updated August 11, 2026

STOCKVEXA stores shop data on your device. MTP CODE does not host your inventory or invoices on a central server. Optional Pro backups live in your own Google Drive.

How to delete your data

  • In-app — delete products, parties, invoices and related records as needed.
  • Uninstall the app — clearing/uninstalling STOCKVEXA removes local app storage on that device.
  • Google Drive backups (Pro) — delete backup files from your Drive if you created them.
  • Exported files — PDFs, CSVs, JSON backups or shares must be deleted manually from those locations.
