STOCKVEXA Privacy Policy

STOCKVEXA – Inventory & Billing · Last updated August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP CODE ("we", "us") handles information in connection with STOCKVEXA (package com.mtpcode.stockvexa), an Android inventory, POS and GST billing app. The app is currently under development and coming soon.

Website: https://mtpcode.com
Support: mtpcode.in@gmail.com

1. Overview

STOCKVEXA is offline-first. Core inventory, sales, purchases, customers, invoices and reports work without an MTP CODE account and without uploading your shop records to MTP CODE servers.

2. Data stored on your device

STOCKVEXA may store the following locally on your device:

  • Business profile (shop name, GSTIN, UPI ID, logo)
  • Products, categories, stock quantities and HSN/SAC
  • Customers and suppliers (names, phones, photos, GSTIN)
  • Sales invoices, purchases, returns, expenses and ledgers
  • Staff roles, PIN/partner lock settings and activity audit log
  • Preferences (theme, backup schedule) and local JSON backups you create

3. What we do not collect

  • No MTP CODE login required for core use
  • No upload of shop records to MTP CODE servers
  • No sale of your inventory or billing data

4. Sharing, export and WhatsApp

If you share invoices (PDF), thermal print, WhatsApp, CSV audit export, or backups, content leaves the app only through the destination you choose. Those third-party apps have their own privacy policies.

5. Google Drive backup (Pro)

Optional Pro cloud backup uses your Google account and Google Drive. Backup files are stored in your Drive under your control. Google’s privacy policy applies to that service. MTP CODE does not host your Drive backups.

6. Advertising

The free version may show Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob may use advertising identifiers according to Google’s policies. Invoice and inventory contents are not sent to AdMob by STOCKVEXA.

7. Purchases (STOCKVEXA Pro)

Optional Pro upgrades are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google, not by MTP CODE. Pro may remove ads and unlock Drive backup features.

8. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — ads, Play Billing, and optional Drive sync
  • Camera / photos — business logo, customer/supplier photos, UPI QR scan (when you use those features)
  • Contacts — optional pick from phone contacts
  • Biometric — optional unlock
  • Storage / share sheet — when you save, share, or restore backups

9. Children’s privacy

STOCKVEXA is a business utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Disclaimer

STOCKVEXA is a shop helper for inventory and billing. It is not a substitute for a chartered accountant, tax filing software, or legal advice. Always verify GST filings and statutory records independently.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when material updates are made.
