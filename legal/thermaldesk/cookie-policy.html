ThermalDesk Cookie Policy

ThermalDesk · Last updated August 7, 2026

ThermalDesk is a native Android app and does not use browser cookies. Any preferences or history it keeps (such as bill history or printer profiles) are stored using Android's local app storage, not cookies, and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

Embedded Web Content

ThermalDesk does not embed third-party web views that would set cookies on your behalf.
