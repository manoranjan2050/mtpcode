ThermalDesk Privacy Policy

ThermalDesk · Last updated August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MTP Code (MANORANJAN) ("we", "us") handles information in connection with ThermalDesk, an Android app published under the MTPCode brand.

Information We Collect

ThermalDesk does not collect or transmit your receipt content or business data to our servers. ThermalDesk is fully offline — there is no account, login, or backend. Saved printer profiles (name, Bluetooth address) are stored locally on your device only.

Permissions

ThermalDesk requests the following permissions, used only for the app's core features:

  • Bluetooth (and, on Android 11 and below, Location — required by Android to perform Bluetooth scans, not used to access or store your actual location) — to discover, connect to, and print on your ESC/POS thermal printer.
  • Camera and Photos — to capture or select an image for the Print Image feature. Images are processed on your device and sent only to your printer, never uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet — used only to serve ads via Google AdMob (see Third-Party Services below). No app data is sent over this connection.

Third-Party Services

ThermalDesk uses Google AdMob to show ads and may share a device advertising identifier with Google for this purpose. See Google’s privacy policy for details on how they process this data.

Data Security

Because all app data stays on your device, the largest security factor is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device updated and access-controlled (e.g. a screen lock).

Children's Privacy

ThermalDesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
