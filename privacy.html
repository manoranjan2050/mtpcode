Privacy Policy

mtpcode.com · Last updated August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers mtpcode.com itself — the website you're reading now. Each app published under the MTPCode brand has its own, more specific privacy policy linked from its app page and listed in the Download Center.

Information We Collect

mtpcode.com is a static website with no backend, no database and no user accounts. We do not run server-side analytics or tracking scripts. If you use the newsletter signup or contact form, the email address or message you submit is sent directly to us (currently via electroiot.in@gmail.com) and is not stored in any database on this site.

Cookies

This site stores a single local preference — your light/dark theme choice — in your browser's local storage. This is not a tracking cookie and is never transmitted anywhere; it only controls how the page looks on your next visit.

Third-Party Links

mtpcode.com links out to GitHub, app stores, and other third-party sites for downloads and source code. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to electroiot.in@gmail.com.
